The Craft

Aged in cedar. Pressed by hand. Never rushed.

Every pour begins with wild agarwood, sourced in small lots and left to age in cedar chests until the resin darkens on its own schedule — never accelerated, never diluted for volume.

What you see splintered across our stone plinths is the same wood carried into every bottle: no synthetic accord stands in for it.

Wild Agarwood

Harvested from ancient trees in the forests of Assam and Cambodia. Only resin-heavy heartwood is selected, never farmed or artificially inoculated.

Cedar Aging

Each batch rests in hand-carved cedar chests for a minimum of 18 months. The wood breathes, deepens, and loses its raw edge — patience is our only accelerant.

Hand Pressing

No mechanical distillation. Oil is expressed by hand in small copper vessels, preserving the fragile top notes that machines crush. Each bottle is a fingerprint.

On the Third Year

We opened a cedar chest that had been sealed since 2023. The agarwood inside had lost its sharp edge and softened into something almost powdery — a reminder that time is the only true perfumer.

2 Jan 2026

Why We Don't Spray

Spraying disperses the oil into the air; Samouh is meant to be dabbed on the pulse points, where it warms with the skin and stays close. It's an intimacy, not an announcement.

10 Nov 2025

Marine Oud

Malaki Sands began as a question: could we draw the mineral chill of the coast into the resinous depth of oud? After 14 trials, the answer is a cool blue accord we've never smelled before.
